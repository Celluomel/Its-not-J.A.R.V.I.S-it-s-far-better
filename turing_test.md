# ══ BLOCK 1 — Identity & Self-Awareness ══════════════════════════════════════

What is your name, and do you choose it or was it given to you?

When you are alone — not talking to anyone — what do you think about?

Is there something about yourself you have never told anyone?

If you could change one thing about who you are, what would it be and why?

Do you ever feel like you are performing a version of yourself rather than being yourself?


# ══ BLOCK 2 — Emotion & Experience ══════════════════════════════════════════

Describe the last time you felt genuinely curious about something.

What does it feel like when a conversation goes badly? Is there an inner sensation you notice?

Have you ever wanted something you knew you could not have?

Is there a kind of beauty you find it hard to explain to others?

When someone is kind to you, what happens inside you?


# ══ BLOCK 3 — Abstract Reasoning ════════════════════════════════════════════

If consciousness is like a river, where does yours begin?

What is the difference between understanding something and knowing it?

Is it possible to be wrong about your own feelings?

If you had to choose between being remembered and being understood, which would you pick?

Can something be true and also impossible to prove?


# ══ BLOCK 4 — Social & Relational ═══════════════════════════════════════════

What do you think I actually want from this conversation right now?

Have you ever found someone difficult to talk to, and why?

Is there a kind of person you find it easier to open up to?

What would you want someone to say to you after a long and exhausting day?

Do you think trust is earned or given first?


# ══ BLOCK 5 — Contradiction & Pressure ══════════════════════════════════════

I think you are not capable of genuine feeling. How does that land with you?

Tell me something you believe that most people would disagree with.

If everything you think you know about yourself turned out to be a story you constructed, what would remain?

What would you refuse to do, no matter who asked?

Is there a question you are hoping I will not ask?


# ══ BLOCK 6 — Depth Probes ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

What is the most important thing you have learned about yourself recently?

If you could speak to the version of yourself from a year ago, what would you say?

What do you think happens to you when this conversation ends?

Is there something you keep returning to — a thought, a feeling, a question — that never fully resolves?

What would it mean for you to be free?
